MINI KIOTVIET - BAN SUA LOI KHOI DONG

1. Sao luu folder plugin cu va export database qua phpMyAdmin truoc khi thay the.
2. Giai nen ZIP; chep folder mini-kiotviet vao wp-content/plugins/ (ghi de file cu).
3. Bat Apache va MySQL trong XAMPP.
4. Vao wp-admin > Plugins. Neu plugin da active, thu tai lai wp-admin.
5. Neu menu khong hien, deactivate va activate lai Mini KiotViet (KHONG xoa database).
6. Thu Dashboard, San pham, Danh muc, Khach hang, Quan ly kho, Don hang, Bao cao.
7. Neu van loi, bat WP_DEBUG_LOG va gui wp-content/debug.log.
